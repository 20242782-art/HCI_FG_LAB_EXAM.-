Me as a Student

My name is Michaelangelo Vergara, and this page is about who I am as a student. "Kunting Bato, Kunting semento munumento" is the quote I try to live by, since every small effort I put in today builds toward something bigger.

Outside of school, I'm a world boxing champion. Balancing training and academics has taught me discipline, time management, and how to stay focused even when things get tough.

As a student, I bring that same discipline into my studies. I believe that consistency, whether in the ring or in the classroom, is what turns small steps into real achievements.
